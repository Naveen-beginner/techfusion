OCTOBER 9, 2026

THE TECHFUSION JOURNEY

Timeline of the whole TECHFUSION event

08:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Prelims

Meta Matrix: Online MCQ round covering basic programming and core computer science fundamentals.
Micro Mission: 15 MCQs covering general reasoning and basic-level programming, followed by selection of six teams for the Mains.
Meme Magic: Online submission of original static memes based on the given themes. Top 25 teams are shortlisted based on creativity, originality, humor, and theme relevance.

01:00 PM – 04:00 PM

Mains

Meta Matrix: Teams solve five coding and analytical problems of varying difficulty. Solutions are evaluated using predefined test cases.
Micro Mission: Qualified teams compete through Think N Sync, Tech Charades, Racking Brains, and Byte Talks, testing technical knowledge, teamwork, reasoning, and com munication.
Meme Magic: Shortlisted teams participate in the Video Meme Challenge, creating a 10–15 second video meme based on a surprise theme.

04:00 PM – 05:00 PM

Final Demos, Judging & Grand Valedictory

Felicitation of winners, followed by the distribution of cash prizes and certificates.
