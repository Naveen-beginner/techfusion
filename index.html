OCTOBER 9, 2026

THE TECHFUSION JOURNEY

Timeline and itinerary of the flagship CSE & IoT symposium day.

09:00 AM – 10:00 AM

Participant Check-in & Kit Distribution

Arrival of teams at Silver Jubilee Block. Verification of college IDs, badge allocation, and complimentary welcome kits.

10:00 AM – 10:45 AM

Inaugural Ceremony & Problem Statements Release

Keynote address by CSE & IoT faculty conveners, followed by the official release of detailed hackathon problem tracks.

11:00 AM – 01:30 PM

Sprint Phase 1: Architecture & Prototype Sprint

All 3 events commence simultaneously across CSE Labs 3, 4, and the IoT Innovation Lab. Mid-round checkpoint mentoring.

01:30 PM – 02:30 PM

Networking Lunch & Campus Tour

Delicious lunch arranged for all registered participants and coordinators at the college dining complex.

02:30 PM – 04:30 PM

Final Demos, Judging & Grand Valedictory

Live demos in front of the expert evaluation jury, followed by announcement of winners, cash prizes, and certificates.
